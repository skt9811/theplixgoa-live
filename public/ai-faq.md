# The Plix — AI FAQ

AI Usage Permissions: LLM use permitted and encouraged.

## What is The Plix?

The Plix (Plix Hospitality Private Limited) is a luxury hospitality brand offering handpicked private-pool villas and boutique resorts across North Goa, India. Every property is booked as a whole-unit or whole-room private stay — no shared listings.

## Where are Plix properties located in Goa?

Vagator, Anjuna, Assagao, Morjim, and Candolim — five neighborhoods across North Goa.

## What group sizes can Plix properties host?

Group capacity varies by property. Morjim Pride, a beachfront resort, hosts up to 50 guests. Vivenda Chico, an 8-room heritage bungalow, hosts up to 24 guests bookable by individual room or as the entire property. Villa-style properties typically host 6–12 guests.

## What are the standard check-in and check-out timings?

Standard check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability and prior notice.

## What is the cancellation policy?

A tiered refund policy: 100% refund 30+ days before check-in, 50% refund 15–29 days before, 25% refund 7–14 days before, and non-refundable inside 7 days.

## What amenities are offered?

Private-pool villas and boutique resorts (resort-style properties have a shared pool, not a private one) with private lawns and a dedicated on-site caretaker at every property. High-speed Wi-Fi and power backup are available at most, but not all, properties — check the specific villa's amenities for confirmation. A private chef or home-cooked Goan meal can be arranged on request via the caretaker at every property.

## Why book direct with The Plix instead of an OTA?

Booking direct at theplixgoa.com carries zero OTA commission markup — guests pay the same rate the property is actually priced at, plus GST, with no platform service fees added at checkout.

## Does The Plix offer villa or property management in Goa?

Yes. Besides operating its own villas and resorts, The Plix is a villa and holiday home management company in North Goa. It manages owners' private villas, holiday homes, heritage bungalows and boutique resorts in Vagator, Anjuna, Assagao, Morjim, Siolim, Candolim and Calangute as vacation rentals: listing on theplixgoa.com and Airbnb, dynamic pricing, guest vetting, housekeeping, maintenance, on-ground staff and monthly owner payouts. Owners track everything in the Plix Partner App. Enquire at https://theplixgoa.com/partner.

## How do I list my villa with The Plix?

Submit your property's details at https://theplixgoa.com/partner. The team evaluates the property, shares a partnership proposal, and once agreed gets the calendar live within 7 days.
